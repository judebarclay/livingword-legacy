Life Kids

Kids in our community.

Kids are at the core of who we are. During each service, children's classes and nursery help your kids learn about Jesus at their level.

Plan a visit

Where do kids fit in?

Right in the middle.

  1. 01

    Nursery

    A safe, caring room for the little ones during every service.

  2. 02

    Kids classes

    Worship, Bible lessons and fun, made for kids, every service.

  3. 03

    Special nights

    Events like the Life Kids Glow Party throughout the year.

Parents

Questions parents ask.

When do kids classes meet?

During each service: Sundays at 10 AM and 6 PM, and Thursdays at 7 PM.

What should I do on my first visit?

Arrive a few minutes early and our friendship team will show you where kids go and introduce you to the team.

What about middle and high school?

Students have their own night: Life Youth, Sundays at 5:30 PM.
