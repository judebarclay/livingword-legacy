Invasion Legacy · 1996–2026

30 YEARS OF INVASION

The 30-year tribute. Tap for sound.

30 years ago, God created a movement.

Not just a place. A legacy.

  1. 1996

    The first Invasion.

    Josh Barclay founded Invasion in Midland, Michigan: a summer conference for young people that became a movement.

  2. 2007

    InvasionX.

    Red light, loud worship and a room that would not sit down.

  3. 2010

    Bigger every summer.

    A whole generation with hands up, and flag football till dark.

  4. 2012

    The friends you met here.

    The people from another youth group who became family by Friday.

  5. 2013

    Days outside, nights inside.

    Giant beach balls in the afternoon. Encounter every night.

  6. 2014

    Heroes.

    Smoke, fire, pyro, light and a room full of voices.

  7. 2015

    I Am.

    Worship that spilled off the stage and into the crowd.

  8. 2016

    Echoes.

    Courts, fields, brackets and bragging rights.

  9. 2017

    Absolute.

    One sentence somebody said that followed you home.

  10. 2018

    Evidence.

    Talent shows, game shows and students who met God.

  11. 2019

    Unrivaled.

    Five-minute sermons, breakout sessions and nights that ran long.

  12. 2024

    Freedom.

    A room full of young people ready to meet God.

  13. 2025

    Revolution.

    Students crying out for revival, night after night.

  14. 2026

    Legacy. Thirty years.

    Three decades of a generation encountering God, celebrated in the room where it started.

More than the stage

Bread bites, soft serve and the friend from another youth group.

Brackets, bad calls and bragging rights. Courts, fields, cards and controllers. Tribes, talent shows and breakout sessions.

Built on those who came before us

Summerall, Osteen, Hix, Sutton, Evans, Barclay.

A torch passed from voice to voice, pulpit to pulpit, father to son. Not asking young people to admire the torch, but to carry it.

That is the legacy

Someone gets saved. Someone gets free. Someone goes under the water and comes back up new. Someone is filled with the Holy Spirit. Someone goes home knowing Jesus is real.

Not 30 years of events. 30 years of people just like you.

You are the legacy

Tonight is where you grab the torch.
