ABOUT
INVASION

For 30 years, Invasion has called generations to surrender their lives to Jesus Christ and fulfill God's purpose for their lives.

Josh Barclay founded Invasion in 1996 and has led it ever since: powerful preaching, dynamic worship, creative media and unforgettable days together. The goal has never been to host an event, but to see lives transformed and revival carried home to churches, schools and communities.

Every summer, youth and young adults from across the country fill Living Word Church in Midland, Michigan for four days of worship, preaching and encounter.

It can't be explained. It has to be experienced.
