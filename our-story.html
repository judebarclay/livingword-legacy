Our story

God is faithful.

From a small rented hall to a home of our own, this is the story of what God has done since 1981.

The call

It started far from Michigan.

While serving in Vietnam, Mark T. Barclay answered God's call to ministry. He went on to Bible college in California, preaching wherever doors opened, before returning to Michigan to plant a church.

1980

Before the building.

Before there was a building there were people hungry for the Word, and new believers baptized in Michigan lakes.

1981

A church is born.

The church was planted in 1981, and those early services met in a rented hall. In May 1981 the congregation gathered to dedicate the church to God.

Building

Room to grow.

As the family grew, so did the vision, and the church built a home of its own.

Today

Who we are today.

Today Living Word Church is a Spirit-filled, non-denominational Word of Faith church with ministry for every age, from infants through high school, young adults and beyond.

2027

Living Word becomes Legacy.

A new name for the same family, carrying more than forty years of faithfulness into the next generation.
