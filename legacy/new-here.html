Our values

What matters to us.

  1. 01

    Love

    We're committed to loving God and loving others, and to being known for our love for one another.

  2. 02

    Worship

    We glorify God through worship rooted in Scripture that draws us closer to Him.

  3. 03

    Service

    We serve our community and those in need through physical and spiritual acts of love and kindness.

  4. 04

    Evangelism

    We spread the good news of Jesus Christ to all people, through both deed and word.

  5. 05

    Discipleship

    We prioritize discipling others, helping them grow in their understanding of the Bible and their relationship with God.

  6. 06

    Stewardship

    We're wise stewards of the resources God has given us, to meet the needs of the church and community.

  7. 07

    Unity

    We foster unity in diversity, standing together as a church family united in sharing God's love.

  8. 08

    Holiness

    We aim to be a holy people, living in obedience to God's Word and surrendered to the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

  9. 09

    Mission

    We fulfill the Great Commission, making disciples of Jesus Christ in our region and beyond.

Our beliefs

What we believe.

  1. 01

    The Trinity

    There is one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

  2. 02

    The Bible

    The Bible is the true and inspired Word of God, our final authority for faith and life.

  3. 03

    Jesus

    Jesus Christ is the Son of God. He died for our sins, rose again and is coming back.

  4. 04

    Salvation

    Salvation is a gift, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

  5. 05

    Baptism

    Water baptism is a public declaration of new life in Christ.

  6. 06

    The Holy Spirit

    The Holy Spirit lives in every believer and empowers us to live for God today.

  7. 07

    The Church

    The Church is the body of Christ, a family of believers called to worship, grow and serve together.

  8. 08

    Prayer

    Prayer changes things. We bring everything to God with faith.
