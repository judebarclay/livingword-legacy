Know Jesus

Who is Jesus?

Jesus is the Son of God. He lived a perfect life, died for our sins and rose again, so anyone who believes in Him can have forgiveness, freedom and eternal life.

  1. 1

    Acknowledge

    Romans 3:23

    We have all sinned and fall short of God's glory. Admit your need for Him.

  2. 2

    Believe

    John 3:16

    God loved the world so much that He gave His Son. Believe that Jesus died and rose again for you.

  3. 3

    Repent & confess

    Acts 3:19

    Turn from sin and turn to God, so your sins can be wiped away.

  4. 4

    Receive

    Revelation 3:20

    Jesus is knocking. Open the door and invite Him into your life.

  5. 5

    Follow

    Luke 9:23

    Follow Jesus every day. You don't have to do it alone; we'd love to walk with you.
