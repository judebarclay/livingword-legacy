Questions

Good questions.

When are services?

Sundays at 10 AM and 6 PM, and Thursdays at 7 PM. Legacy Youth meets Sundays at 5:30 PM.

Where are you, and where do I park?

2010 N Stark Rd, Midland, MI 48642. We have a large on-site parking lot right in front of the building. Open in Maps.

How long is a service?

About 90 minutes, with worship and a message from the Bible.

What should I wear?

We love to dress up for worship, but we want you to feel comfortable. Come as you are.

What about my kids?

Children's classes and nursery run during each service, so your kids learn about Jesus at their level while you worship. See Legacy Kids.

Is there something for teens?

Yes. Legacy Youth meets Sundays at 5:30 PM for worship, fellowship and fun. See Legacy Youth.

Is the building accessible?

Yes, the building is wheelchair accessible.

What kind of church is this?

We're a Spirit-filled, non-denominational Word of Faith church. Read what we believe.

Can I watch online?

Every service streams live on YouTube and in the Legacy App. When a service is live, this site pops the stream up for you. See Watch.

Can I talk to a pastor?

Yes. Our Flock Care Minister, Ryan Schneider, is here for you: 989-859-8045.

How do I ask for prayer?

Call the prayer line at 989-835-4562 and leave a voicemail, email prayer@livingword.ws, or use the prayer request form.

How do I get baptized or become a member?

Sign up for water baptism or the membership class. Both are listed on Next Steps.

How can I give?

Give online any time through Pushpay, in the Legacy App, or during any service. See Give.

Who do I contact with other questions?

Call the office at 989-832-7547 or email info@mbmmail.com.

See you Sunday.

Questions first? Call 989-832-7547 or email info@mbmmail.com.
